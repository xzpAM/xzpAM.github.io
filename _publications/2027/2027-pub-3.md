abstract: >-
  AetherVLA is a Vision-Language-Action (VLA) system for long-horizon humanoid manipulation. Rather than attaching a generic external VLM directly to an action head, AetherVLA builds on our own vision-language backbone that has already undergone action-aware training, and redesigns the policy head, training objectives, and real-robot alignment pipeline on top of it. The system adopts a two-level action generation structure: the upper-level HRLF (Hierarchical Receding-Horizon Latent Flow) extracts coarse-grained trajectory plans from vision, language, and robot state, while the lower-level flow action expert generates fine-grained action chunks conditioned on these plans. This design preserves the verified convergence properties of flow matching while maintaining stage consistency across long-horizon execution. The demo shows whole-body mobile control on a Unitree G1 humanoid robot.
